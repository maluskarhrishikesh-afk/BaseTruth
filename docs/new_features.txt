Here’s how each feature is actually captured in real systems.

1. Motion Realism
These features analyze whether movement behaves like a real human head.

A. Head Velocity Variance
What it means
How quickly the head movement speed changes over time.

Real humans:

accelerate/decelerate naturally

movement is not perfectly linear

Replay attacks:

often smoother or robotic

How to capture
Track:

yaw

pitch

roll

for every frame.

Example:

velocity_yaw = yaw[t] - yaw[t-1]
Then compute variance:

np.var(velocity_series)
Higher natural variance = more human.

B. Acceleration Smoothness
What it means
Whether movement acceleration looks biomechanically realistic.

How to calculate
First:

velocity = pos[t] - pos[t-1]
Then:

acceleration = velocity[t] - velocity[t-1]
Then measure:

jerk

acceleration spikes

smoothing

Humans:

smooth acceleration curves

Fake video:

frame jumps

unnatural discontinuities

C. Motion Entropy
What it means
Randomness/unpredictability in movement.

How to capture
Create sequence:

[yaw1, yaw2, yaw3 ...]
Measure entropy:

Low entropy:

Normal entropy:

human micro-adjustments
