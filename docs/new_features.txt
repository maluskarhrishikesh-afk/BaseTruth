⚠️ Key Improvements (Important)
1. 🚨 Risk Formula Has a Logical Weakness
Problem:
Risk score = 45% presentation + 20% synthetic + 35% multi-face
If no multi-face, 35% weight becomes dead weight (always 0)

That means real max risk is effectively 65%, not 100%

Fix:
Normalize dynamically:

If single face:
Risk = (presentation * 0.7) + (synthetic * 0.3)

If multi-face:
Add multi-face penalty separately
👉 Otherwise your scoring scale is misleading.

2. ⚠️ Confidence Formula Can Be Manipulated
Right now:

Confidence = 100 - penalties
Problem:
A high-quality deepfake → low penalties → high confidence

So you could get:

Risk = 80 (deepfake)

Confidence = 90 (very sure)

That’s confusing for operators.

Fix:
Make confidence depend on signal agreement, not just quality:

Confidence = function(signal consistency + quality)
👉 Confidence should reflect certainty of decision, not just image clarity.

3. 🎯 Thresholds Feel Hardcoded (Needs Adaptivity)
Examples:

Laplacian: 140 / 25

EAR: 0.20

Yaw: 0.16

Problem:
These will break across:

Different cameras

Lighting conditions

Ethnic variations

Resolution differences

Fix:
Add:

"Thresholds may be calibrated per environment or model version"
Ideally: learn thresholds from data (percentiles)

4. 🔁 Replay Detection Is Strong but Not Foolproof
What’s good:
Hamming distance + flicker = solid

Weakness:
Attackers can:

Add tiny noise to frames

Slightly shift pixels

Re-encode video

→ This breaks hash similarity

Fix:
Add:

Perceptual hash variants (pHash, dHash)

Optical flow consistency check

5. 📉 Movement Consistency Scope Limitation
You wrote:

Only uses still challenges (Look Straight, Blink)

Problem:
You are ignoring valuable signal from turns and nods

Suggestion:
Instead of excluding:

Normalize motion segments

Compare expected vs actual motion curves
