Technical Validation of Current Logic
The logic behind your 22 combined layers is grounded in standard forensic practices. Below is a breakdown of why your core layers are effective:

1. Image Engine Highlights
Layer 1 (ELA)
Correctly identifies compression ghosting. Re-saving at 75% is an industry-standard method for highlighting non-synchronous compression artifacts.
Layer 5 (DCT)
This is mathematically one of the most reliable ways to detect double-compression in JPEGs. The comb pattern in the histogram is a definitive artifact of the re-quantization process.
Layer 11 (FFT Spike)
Excellent for detecting GAN-generated or diffusion-model images. The periodicity left by upsampling layers in AI models creates unmistakable frequency-domain spikes.

2. PDF Engine Highlights
Layer 1 (Incremental Updates)
This is your smoking gun. Detecting multiple %%EOF markers is one of the most reliable ways to prove a PDF was modified after creation, without needing complex AI.
Layer 7 (Digital Signatures)
Your use of ByteRange analysis is critical. Checking for uncovered bytes after the signature is the only reliable way to detect Signature Wrapping attacks.

Suggested Improvements
Your current logic is already strong, but there are several ways to harden the system against sophisticated pro-sumer forgers.

1. Shift from Manual Scoring to ML Classification
You are currently using fixed point values (for example: +25 points for ELA).
Risk:
This can lead to point creep, where multiple harmless artifacts (such as mobile scanning metadata + low entropy) incorrectly trigger a TAMPERED verdict.
Fix:
Feed the raw outputs of all 11 layers into a Random Forest or XGBoost classifier.
This allows the model to learn patterns such as:


ELA is only meaningful if metadata anomalies also exist


DCT + font mismatch together are stronger than either alone


Minor noise alone should not raise alerts



2. Implement Font Geometry Analysis
Your current Layer 10 checks stroke width and sharpness.
Improvement:
Add Baseline Alignment Checks.
Forgers often struggle to align pasted text perfectly on the vertical axis.
Example:


Word Salary baseline at y = 400


Amount next to it baseline at y = 401.5


That mismatch becomes a high-probability forgery indicator.

3. PRNU (Photo Response Non-Uniformity)
Your Layer 4 uses noise residuals, which is already good for finding patchwork edits.
Improvement:
If you receive multiple related documents (such as PAN card + selfie), use PRNU fingerprinting to verify whether both came from the same physical camera sensor.
This helps prevent identity mismatching where unrelated images are submitted together.

4. Advanced PDF Shadow Attack Detection
A common professional PDF trick is to place a visual mask over original text so the old content still exists in the file.
Improvement:
Scan for Overlapping Text Objects.
If two text strings share the same coordinates (x, y) but one is layered above the other, flag it as a potential Shadow Edit.
